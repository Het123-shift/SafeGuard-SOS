Slide 02 / Problem Statement

The Critical Problem: Why Conventional Safety Apps Fail Under Duress

1. High Panic Latency
Cognitive & physical paralysis
  • Victims cannot unlock screens or dial numbers under physical assault.
  • Over 70% of victims are physically unable to articulate GPS coordinates.
  • Traditional apps require complex multi-step screen navigation.
2. The Connectivity Trap
Single-point-of-failure networking
  • Cloud-only alerts stall when 4G/5G drops in basements, lifts or transit.
  • WhatsApp deep-links fail without active data packets.
  • Responders without the proprietary app get zero telemetry.
3. Tampered Evidence
Loss of critical chain of custody
  • Phones are routinely snatched or smashed by perpetrators.
  • Unencrypted voice/photo evidence is permanently destroyed.
  • Guardians lack secure emergency recovery keys.

Slide 05 / Engineering Design

Transparent Engineering Analysis: Intentional Fallback Matrix

1. Geolocation: Last-Known Location Fallback
Prevents 15s satellite GPS stall delays
  • Current Behavior: Transmits immediate verified Last Known Location.
  • Why it's a Fallback: Prevents emergency dispatch stalling in basements/elevators while waiting for satellite fixes.
2. Connectivity: Native SIM SMS Fallback
Graceful degradation under zero data
  • Current Behavior: WhatsApp requires active internet packets.
  • Why it's a Fallback: Automatically delegates to direct cellular SIM SMS when data is disconnected.
3. Vault Security: Master Passkey Access
Cryptographic local protection
  • Current Behavior: Vault audio is heavily encrypted locally.
  • Why it's a Fallback: Requires guardian/parent passkey to prevent scene attacker tampering.
4. Sensor & Hardware Baseline Thresholds
Conservative calibration to prevent false alarms
  • Current Behavior: Volume 3-tap in-app; higher G-force spike threshold.
  • Why it's a Fallback: Eliminates accidental panic dispatches during jogging or handling.

Slide 07 / Bottlenecks & Choke Points

Where It Chokes: Honest Assessment of Current MVP Gaps

1. Cold-Start Latency
App boot time bottleneck
  • Takes too much time to initialize JS bundle on cold launch.
  • Need sub-second instant readiness when opened under duress.
2. Static vs Live Tracking
Telemetry continuity
  • Transmits victim's Last Known Location rather than a smooth continuous live GPS stream.
  • Needs real-time polyline breadcrumbs for moving victims.
3. Offline WhatsApp Limit
Internet dependency
  • WhatsApp deep-links fail without active data packets.
  • Relies entirely on single-channel Native SIM SMS when offline.
4. Evidence Vault Decryption Barrier

Audio files are heavily encrypted locally. Family members/parents cannot access recordings remotely without physical access and knowing the victim's device passkey.

5. OS Lock-Screen Hook & High Fall Force

Android OS blocks 3-tap volume key events when phone screen is locked. Also, fall detection sensitivity threshold is too high (requires intense collision spike).
